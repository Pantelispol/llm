## History

Modiano Market is a covered food market in Thessaloniki’s central commercial district. The hall belongs to the city’s early twentieth-century urban fabric and reflects the importance of organized food trade near Aristotelous Square and the port. Restoration and reuse have retained the market identity while adapting the interior for contemporary vendors and visitors.

## What to see

The long hall, roof structure, repeated shop bays, signs, and central circulation route define the space. Food counters and displays make the visit sensory, but the building itself is also worth observing: its enclosure offers a different market experience from the open lanes of nearby Kapani. Views along the interior emphasize the regular structure and the density of small businesses sharing one civic-scale building.

## Practical tips

Modiano is an active commercial venue rather than a museum. Individual vendors may follow different arrangements, and the descriptive document should not be used to infer whether a specific counter is operating. Central aisles can become crowded, especially when groups stop to browse. Visitors with mobility needs should verify current entrances and interior circulation after any layout changes. Aristotelous Square, Kapani Market, the Jewish Museum, and Ladadika are all nearby, making the hall a convenient sheltered pause in a central route.
