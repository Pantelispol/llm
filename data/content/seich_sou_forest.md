## History

Seich Sou is the peri-urban forest on the hills north and northeast of Thessaloniki. It provides a green backdrop to the city and a landscape for walking, viewpoints, and outdoor recreation. The forest has also been affected by wildfire and remains closely connected with fire prevention, land management, and civil-protection decisions.

## What to see

Routes through the forest offer changing views over Thessaloniki, the Thermaic Gulf, and surrounding hills. The experience is primarily landscape-based: wooded slopes, clearings, ridgelines, and trail junctions replace the concentrated features of an urban monument. Conditions differ substantially between trailheads and paths, so a single map point should never be treated as a safe or complete route.

## Practical tips

This is a safety-critical outdoor destination. Do not plan a visit during storms, after dark, in extreme heat, or when fire-risk restrictions or emergency instructions apply. A known trailhead, suitable footwear, water, daylight, and a route appropriate to the group are essential. Travelers should rely on current official civil-protection and forest information for access decisions. Families, visitors with limited mobility, and anyone unfamiliar with the terrain should choose a conservative route or a lower-risk urban alternative.
